The problem

The doctor comes to you.Your medicine should too.

A prescription can be written locally.

Getting it filled can mean hours on the road — or finding someone else to make the trip.

MILKRUN flies straight to your community.

The run

Four handoffs. One less journey.

01

Pharmacy packs it.

The prescription is dispensed, labelled and sealed.

02

MILKRUN moves it by air.

A long-range VTOL carries the package to a designated community site.

03

A local operator receives it.

The package is removed, recorded and secured.

04

The patient collects it.

Locally.

Pilot flights subject to route-specific aviation, healthcare and community approvals.

Operated locally

A local job at every landing site.

As the network grows, local training could extend into RPAS operations and aviation roles.

Each route could support a paid local operator.

  • Receive deliveries
  • Record the handoff
  • Secure packages
  • Manage the landing site
